## Codebase Patterns
- Always add new model files to Xcode project.pbxproj in 4 places: PBXBuildFile, PBXFileReference, PBXGroup (Models), PBXSourcesBuildPhase
- Register SwiftData models in BookiApp.swift schema array
- Use `swiftc -parse <file>` for syntax validation (full builds require Xcode IDE)
