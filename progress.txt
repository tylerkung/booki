## Codebase Patterns
- Services are caseless `enum` with `static` methods (stateless namespace pattern)
- P/L calculations use `LiabilityService.calculatePayout(stake:odds:)` — returns profit only, not stake back
- Bet.player and LedgerEntry.player are optional relationships (`Player?`) — always use `?.id` for comparison
- `BalanceService.balanceOwed(from:)` takes `[LedgerEntry]` — positive = player owes bookie
- Xcode IDE required for full typecheck — SwiftData macros can't resolve via CLI
