The Problem with Manual Ledger Tracking

Spreadsheets Break Down

Google Sheets and Excel lack the atomic transactions and idempotency controls needed for reliable ledger management. Formulas break, rows get deleted, and one bad edit throws off every balance in your book. Our spreadsheet template guide shows both the potential and the limits.

Settlement Takes Hours

Manual settlement reconciliation — calculating who owes what each week across dozens of picks — is tedious and error-prone. A single miscalculation creates disputes, confusion, and eroded trust with your members.

No Audit Trail

Spreadsheets have no built-in audit trail — anyone with access can alter records without detection. When a member disputes a balance, you have no cryptographic proof that past entries are unmodified.

No Credit Controls

Without automated credit limit enforcement, members overextend before you realize it. Manual tracking makes real-time bankroll management impossible — picks go through unchecked, and you absorb the risk.

How Booki's Ledger Works for You

01

Auto-Settlement

Picks are graded and balances update the moment games finish through atomic transactions. No manual calculations, no end-of-week scramble. Every settlement reconciliation is instant, accurate, and produces an immutable ledger entry.

02

Real-Time Balance Tracking

Every member's running balance updates in real time as picks are graded and settlements clear. Members check their own balance anytime from any browser — eliminating "what do I owe?" texts and reducing your operational overhead to zero.

03

Credit Limit Enforcement

Set per-member credit limits that are enforced automatically at pick submission time. When a member hits their limit, the system blocks new picks until they settle up. Win limit controls add a second layer of bankroll management to cap net winnings per member.

04

Tamper-Evident Hash-Chain Audit Trail

Every transaction is recorded in a hash-chain audit trail where each ledger entry is cryptographically linked to the previous one. This makes it impossible to alter past records without detection — providing dispute-proof, tamper-evident history for every pick and settlement.
