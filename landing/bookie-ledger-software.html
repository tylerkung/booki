The Problem with Manual Ledger Tracking

Spreadsheets Break Down

Google Sheets and Excel weren't designed for ledger management. Formulas break, rows get deleted, and one bad edit can throw off every balance in your book. Our spreadsheet template guide shows both the potential and the limits.

Settlement Takes Hours

Manually calculating who owes what each week is tedious and error-prone. One mistake means disputes, confusion, and lost trust with your members.

No Audit Trail

When a member disputes a balance, can you prove every transaction? Spreadsheets have no built-in audit trail, and changes can be made without anyone knowing.

No Credit Controls

Without credit limits, members can overextend themselves before you realize it. Manual tracking makes it nearly impossible to enforce limits in real time.
