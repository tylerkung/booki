The Problem with Traditional Pay Per Head Services

Per-Head Fees Add Up Fast

Traditional PPH software charges you for every active member each week — whether they place picks or not. With 20 members at $10/head, that is $800/month before your book even turns a profit. See our PPH vs manual ledger comparison for a detailed cost breakdown.

Outdated Interfaces

Most pay per head platforms have desktop-only layouts from a decade ago. Members expect a fast, responsive experience on any device — and clunky interfaces drive them to competitors.

Privacy Concerns

Your entire member list, their pick activity, and your financial data live on a third-party PPH provider's servers. You have no control over their data practices, uptime, or continuity.

Manual Grading & Errors

Many pay per head services still require manual grading or run unreliable automation that misses edge cases. A single grading error cascades into incorrect settlement reconciliation, eroded trust, and lost money.

How Booki Solves Each Problem

01

No Per-Head Fees

Booki uses flat-rate pricing instead of per-member billing. Add 5 members or 50 — your cost stays the same. Predictable overhead means better bankroll management and no surprise charges as your group grows.

02

Simple to Start, Modern UI

Booki runs as a responsive web platform where members browse games with real-time line movement, submit picks against live odds, and check balances — all from any browser on any device.

03

You Own Your Data

Your member list, pick history, and ledger belong to you — not a third-party vendor. Booki is a tool you control with full data portability and zero vendor lock-in.

04

Automated Grading & Settlement

Picks are graded and settled the moment games finish — moneyline, spread, totals, and parlay tracking all handled through atomic transactions with no manual input.
