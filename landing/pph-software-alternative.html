The Problem with Traditional Pay Per Head Services

Per-Head Fees Add Up Fast

Traditional PPH software charges you for every active member each week. With 20 members, that's hundreds per month — whether they place picks or not. See our PPH vs manual ledger comparison for a detailed cost breakdown.

Outdated Interfaces

Most pay per head platforms look like they were built in 2005. Clunky desktop layouts, confusing navigation, and no real mobile experience for your members.

Privacy Concerns

You're trusting a third-party PPH provider with your entire member list, their activity, and your financial data. You don't control the platform — they do.

Manual Grading & Errors

Many pay per head services still require manual grading or have unreliable automation. One mistake in settlement can cost you trust — and money.

How Booki Solves Each Problem

01

No Per-Head Fees

Booki uses flat-rate pricing, not per-member billing. Add 5 members or 50 — your cost stays the same. No surprise charges when your group grows.

02

Mobile-First, Modern UI

Built as a native iOS app, Booki gives your members a clean, intuitive interface to browse games, submit picks, and check balances — all from their phone.

03

You Own Your Data

Your member list, their picks, your ledger — it's all yours. Booki is a tool you control, not a platform that controls you. No vendor lock-in.

04

Auto-Grading & Auto-Settlement

When games finish, picks are graded and settled automatically. Moneyline, spread, and totals — all handled without manual input. Zero errors, zero disputes. Learn how this works in our ledger software overview.
