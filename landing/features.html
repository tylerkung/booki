Platform Features

Everything you need to
run your book.

From pick submission to settlement, Booki handles every step. Here's what's under the hood.

01

Member Management

Add members, set individual credit limits, and control what each member can access. Archive or remove members without losing historical data. Every member gets their own clean interface to browse games and submit picks.

  • Invite links with one-tap onboarding
  • Per-member credit limits & balance tracking
  • Tier-based feature control
  • Archive or remove without data loss
02

Pick Tracking

Singles, multi-picks, alternate lines, and futures. Members submit picks through a clean mobile interface. Every pick is timestamped, odds-locked, and tracked from submission through settlement.

  • Straights, multi-picks & futures
  • Moneyline, spread & totals
  • Alternate lines support
  • Odds locked at submission time
03

Live Odds & Games

Games from NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, and college sports sync automatically with real-time lines. Members see a clean game board organized by sport with live odds on every market.

  • NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL & college sports
  • Real-time odds updates
  • Sport-tabbed game board
  • Search by team name
04

Auto-Grading

When games finish, picks are graded automatically. Moneyline, spread, and totals are all handled. No manual input. Catch-up grading handles any missed events, and stale pending picks are voided automatically.

  • Automatic grading on final scores
  • Moneyline, spread & totals logic
  • Catch-up grading for missed events
  • Stale pick auto-voiding
05

Settlement & Balances

Settlements are calculated automatically when picks are graded. Balances update in real time. Members always see a clear, transparent view of what they owe or are owed—no ambiguity, no disputes. Learn more about how Booki's ledger works.

  • Automatic ledger entries on settlement
  • Real-time balance updates
  • Full transaction history
  • Manual balance adjustments with audit trail
06

Analytics Dashboard

See your operation at a glance. Open activity, exposure alerts, member watchlists, win/loss records, and performance trends. Drill into individual member analytics for deeper insight.

  • Exposure tracking & risk alerts
  • Member-level performance analytics
  • Win/loss records & trends
  • Open activity monitoring
