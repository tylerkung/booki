# Booki vs PerHead.CR: A Ledger vs a Full Sportsbook

PerHead.CR advertises [$7 per active player per week](https://perhead.cr/). That number looks small until it multiplies. Twenty active players is about **$607 a month**. Fifty is about **$1,517**. Booki Pro is **$49.99 a month** for up to 50 members, or **$599.88 a year**. At twenty players, PerHead.CR runs **$7,280 a year** against Booki's $599.88. The gap is not a rounding error. It is the price of renting a sportsbook operation when a lot of independent organizers only needed a ledger.

This is not like-for-like. Read the split below before you switch, or before you stay.

## Who should stay on PerHead.CR

Stay if you are running a book as a business. PerHead.CR's $7 rate includes a full sportsbook, live betting, a prop builder, an online casino, a 285+ track racebook, and a multilingual call center. Live dealer is extra on a 10% profit-share. Premium casino is extra on a 15% profit-share.

If players you do not know personally need to phone in a wager, play slots, or bet horses, that stack is the product. $7 a head to not build any of it yourself is a fair price for that job. Booki will not replace it.

## Who should switch to Booki

Switch if you organize for people you already know (roughly 8 to 50 members), you already settle outside the software, and nobody is using the casino, racebook, or call center. That is the case where per-head billing stops making sense. Not because PerHead.CR is overpriced, but because you are buying an operation when you needed a record.

Booki is pick-tracking and bankroll management software. Brand line: **Be The House.** It is not a sportsbook. It does not process payments or handle funds. There is no casino, no racebook, no call center, and no full branded sportsbook website. The cap is 50 members. Past that, this comparison is not worth making.

If you needed the house, stay. If you needed a ledger, you are overpaying.

4. Set credit limits and win limits. Leave historical picks where they are. New picks go on Booki against live odds and grade automatically.
5. Keep settling the way you already do. Booki records the balance. It never touches the money.
