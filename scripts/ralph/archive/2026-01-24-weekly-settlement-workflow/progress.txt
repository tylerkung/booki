## Codebase Patterns
- SwiftData @Model classes need @Attribute(.unique) for id fields
- Use Theme.swift for all styling (Theme.background, Theme.cardBackground, Theme.accent, Theme.danger, etc.)
- Services are typically enums with static functions, or classes for stateful services (ObservableObject)
- Views go in Booki/Views/, Services in Booki/Services/, Models in Booki/Models/
- New files must be added to project.pbxproj in 4 places: PBXBuildFile, PBXFileReference, PBXGroup, PBXSourcesBuildPhase
- Use `contains(where:)` not `contains {}` for closure-based contains checks
- BookiApp.swift is the main app entry point with modelContainer and environment setup
- CollectionStatus enum in Player.swift: .noStatus, .reminded, .promised, .overdue
- PaymentMethod enum exists in PaymentSheet.swift (cash, venmo, zelle, etc.)
